Privacy Policy

Currency Converter + Units · Last updated 3 October 2026

Short version: this app does not collect your personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Currency Converter + Units ("the app") is developed and published by Ikramul Murad ("we", "us"). You can reach us at currencyconverter26@outlook.com.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, receive or store any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address
  • Any account or login — the app has none
  • Payment card or billing information
  • Your location
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or any cross-app tracking identifier

The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. Because we do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

3. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, so that it works the way you left it. This data never leaves your device, and we have no way to read it.

What is storedWhy
Recently downloaded exchange ratesSo conversion keeps working without an internet connection
Your default base currencySo you do not have to pick it every time
Your selected currency list and saved favouritesTo restore your list when you reopen the app
App preferences (display, refresh interval, speech speed)To keep your settings
A daily counter of free conversionsTo apply the free usage limit

You can erase all of it at any time: use Settings → Reset to Defaults inside the app, or delete the app from your device, which removes everything it stored.

4. How exchange rates reach the app

The app does not contact the rate providers directly. It asks a small server we run — a Google Cloud Function hosted in Singapore — which fetches current rates and caches them, so that every user is served from one shared copy instead of each device querying the providers separately.

That server collects the rates from:

  • the Currency API, delivered through the jsDelivr CDN
  • ExchangeRate-API, used as a fallback if the first request fails

Because the fetching happens on our server, the providers see requests from us, not from you.

The request your app makes to our server contains only a currency code — no name, no account, no device identifier, and nothing you typed into the app. We keep no user records and no database of people: there is nothing stored that could say which request came from whom.

As with any web server, ours automatically records technical request logs that include the IP address a request came from. This is standard infrastructure logging handled by Google Cloud, kept for a short period and then discarded. We do not read it to identify anyone, do not link it to a person, do not use it for analytics, advertising or tracking, and do not share or sell it.

5. Maps

The "Nearby ATMs" and "Nearby Currency Exchange" options do not use your location. Tapping them opens Apple Maps or Google Maps with a text search such as "Nearby ATMs". Anything that happens after that — including how those apps use your location — is covered by Apple's or Google's privacy policy, not ours.

6. Subscriptions and payments

Optional Pro subscriptions are sold and processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Payment happens inside Apple's system: we never see or receive your card details, billing address or Apple Account information. The app only receives a yes-or-no answer from Apple about whether a valid subscription is active. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

7. Speech

The read-aloud feature uses the speech voices built into iOS to speak results out loud. It only produces sound — it does not listen, does not use the microphone, and records nothing.

8. Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. Since we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect it from children either.

9. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR (Europe and the UK) and the CCPA (California) give you the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal data a company holds about you. Apart from the short-lived server logs described in section 4, we hold none, so there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase on your behalf. The data described in section 3 is on your device and fully under your control. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not have any.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on the App Store.
