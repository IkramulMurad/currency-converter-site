2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, receive or store any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or postal address (unless you choose to email us, in which case we receive your email address and what you write)

The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. Because we do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, the app never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

4. How exchange rates reach the app

The app does not contact the rate providers directly. It asks a small server we run: a Cloudflare Worker. Cloudflare, Inc. acts as our hosting provider and processor. The server fetches rates and caches them, so every user is served from one shared copy.

The server uses these sources, in this order:

  • ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com): the primary source of live rates
  • Currency API (fawazahmed0, served via Cloudflare and jsDelivr): the backup source for live rates, the source for precious metals and crypto, and the only source for rate history charts
  • European Central Bank reference rates: used only on the server as a cross-check. A rate table more than 5% off the ECB is rejected. ECB figures are never shown to you.

All three are called by our server, never by your device. They see requests from us, not from you. The widget fetches rates the same way.

A rate request contains only a currency code. It has no identifier and nothing you typed into the app.

Like any web service, Cloudflare automatically records technical request logs that include the IP address a request came from. We keep these logs only for a few days (Cloudflare Workers Logs). We use them only to run and protect the service. To limit abuse, our server briefly counts requests per IP address, and this count is not stored. We do not use the logs to identify anyone, for analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not share or sell them.

5. Rate alerts and notifications

This section applies if you create a rate alert. We then store the following on our server, in a Cloudflare D1 database. If you buy Pro, the install ID and the secret hash are stored there too, so the purchase can be linked to your device (section 6):

What is storedWhy
A random install ID created on your device, and a one-way hash of a random secret. Your device sends the secret with each request to prove it is yours; our server stores only the hash.So only your device can see or change its alerts
Your Apple push notification token, only if you allow notificationsTo deliver the alert
Each alert's currency pair, target rate and direction (above or below), whether it repeats, and when it last firedTo check it against rates every hour

We do not store your name, email, Apple Account or location. The install ID is not written to server logs. Push notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service, and Apple's privacy policy applies to it.

Retention. Alerts stay until you delete them. A one-time alert is deleted after it fires. Data for a device that never allowed notifications is deleted automatically after 30 days. If Apple reports that a push token is no longer valid, for example because the app was deleted, that device's data is removed.

If the service ends. If we stop running the server, for example because the app is discontinued, we will delete the data held on it, including alerts, push registrations, install credentials and purchase records. We will not sell it, and we will not transfer it for any other use. If the app and its service are handed over to a successor, the data is transferred only so the same service can continue under this policy.

6. Subscriptions and payments

Purchases are made and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your card details, billing address or Apple Account information.

If you buy Pro (a subscription or Pro Lifetime), the app sends our server Apple's signed proof of purchase. The server checks Apple's signature. It keeps only the original transaction ID, the product, the expiry date and whether it was refunded. Each time the app refreshes your Pro status, it also sends your install ID and secret, so the purchase can be linked to this install. These are linked to the install IDs that use the purchase, at most 10 devices per purchase. We use this only to decide how many rate alerts you can have: 1 free, up to 50 with Pro. These purchase records are kept while a device uses them. Delete Alerts & Server Data removes this device's link, and the record itself if no other device uses it. A record of a refunded or revoked purchase is kept, with no link to any device, so that deleting data cannot undo a refund.

Apple also notifies our server when a subscription renews, expires or is refunded (App Store Server Notifications). These notices contain no payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

7. Maps

The "Nearby ATMs" and "Nearby Currency Exchange" options do not use your location. Tapping them opens Apple Maps or Google Maps with a text search such as "Nearby ATMs". Anything that happens after that, including how those apps use your location, is covered by Apple's or Google's privacy policy, not ours.

8. Speech

The read-aloud feature uses the speech voices built into iOS to speak results out loud. It only produces sound. It does not listen, does not use the microphone, and records nothing.

9. Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Deleting your data and your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR (Europe and the UK) and the CCPA (California) give you the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal data a company holds about you.

Data on your device is under your control (see section 3). For data on our server, use Settings → Delete Alerts & Server Data (in the Rate Alerts section). This immediately deletes your alerts, push registration, install credential and purchase links from our server. The one exception is the record of a refunded or revoked purchase, which has no link to any device and is kept (section 6). The app then forgets its install ID. You can also email us at currencyconverter26@outlook.com.

We do not sell or share personal information.
